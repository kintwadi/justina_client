Our Story

A journey of passion, craft, and timeless leather

Where It All Began

"Every stitch tells a story. Every piece carries a piece of our soul."

Atelier Justinazaqueu was born from a simple belief: that beautiful things should be made to last. In a world of fast fashion and disposable goods, we chose a different path — one rooted in tradition, patience, and an unwavering commitment to craft.

What began as a one-person workshop has grown into a small atelier of dedicated artisans, each sharing the same passion for creating leather goods that become part of their owner's story. We believe in slow craftsmanship, in the beauty of imperfection, and in the quiet luxury of something made by hand.

The First Stitch

Founded in a small Brussels workshop with nothing but a passion for leather and a dream of creating timeless pieces. The first collection — just five wallets — sold out in a single week through word of mouth alone.

2016
2018

The First Atelier

Moved into our first dedicated workshop space in the heart of Brussels. Expanded the collection to include bags and accessories, and welcomed our first apprentice to the team — beginning a tradition of passing knowledge forward.

International Recognition

Featured in leading design publications across Europe. Our pieces began reaching customers in over 20 countries, with a growing reputation for exceptional craftsmanship and timeless design.

2020
2022

Sustainable Commitment

Launched our sustainability initiative, partnering exclusively with ethical tanneries and introducing our repair-for-life program. Every piece we make is now built to last generations, not seasons.

The Heritage Collection

Released our first limited edition Heritage Series — a celebration of traditional techniques and rare leathers. Each piece numbered, each one a testament to the craft that defines us.

2024
2026

The Present Chapter

Today, our atelier continues to grow while staying true to our founding principles. Each piece is still made by hand, each stitch still placed with intention. The journey continues, one beautiful piece at a time.

The Craft

Every piece goes through a meticulous process, taking up to 20 hours of dedicated craftsmanship

Selection

We personally source each hide from ethical tanneries, selecting only the finest full-grain leather with character and soul.

Cutting

Each piece is hand-cut with precision, honoring the natural character and grain of the leather — no two pieces are ever identical.

Stitching

Traditional saddle stitching by hand — stronger and more durable than any machine can achieve. Each stitch placed with intention.

Finishing

Burnished edges, polished brass hardware, and final inspection ensure every piece meets our exacting standards before it leaves the atelier.

Frequently Asked

How long does shipping take?+
Standard shipping within Europe takes 3-5 business days. International orders typically arrive within 7-10 business days. All orders include tracking and signature confirmation. Complimentary shipping on orders over €150.
Do you offer custom orders?+
Yes! We love creating bespoke pieces. Custom orders typically take 4-6 weeks and begin with a consultation to discuss your vision, leather choices, and personalization options. Contact us to start the conversation.
What is your repair policy?+
Every piece comes with a lifetime repair guarantee. Whether it's a loose stitch, worn edge, or hardware replacement, we'll restore your piece to its former glory — often free of charge for craftsmanship issues.
How do I care for my leather?+
Full-grain leather develops character with age. We recommend occasional conditioning with a quality leather balm, avoiding prolonged sun exposure, and storing in the dust bag we provide. Each piece includes a detailed care guide.
Can I visit the workshop?+
Absolutely. We welcome visitors by appointment Tuesday through Saturday. It's a wonderful opportunity to see the craft in action, feel our leathers, and discuss pieces in person. Book through our contact form.
What is your return policy?+
We offer a 30-day return window for unused items in original condition. Custom orders are final sale. If anything about your piece isn't perfect, please reach out — we're committed to your complete satisfaction.
